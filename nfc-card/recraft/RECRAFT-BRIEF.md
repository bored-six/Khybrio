# Khybrio NFC card: Recraft brief for Khybi art

Drop the exported PNGs (transparent background, 2000px+ on the long side) into this folder:
  Documents/Khybrio/nfc-card/recraft/

Recraft settings (same for every image)
- Style: Vector illustration > Flat / Line art style (not "Realistic", not "Digital illustration")
- Background: transparent
- Export: PNG, 2000px or larger (SVG export too if the plan allows)

Style preamble (paste at the start of every prompt)
Flat vector illustration, single-color silhouette style like a hand-cut sticker, thick rounded shapes, no gradients, no shading, no outlines, no texture, transparent background. Character: Khybi, a small chibi mascot with vinyl-figurine proportions, big rounded head, two tall rounded ears with colored tips, a lighter rounded face patch with closed happy eyes and a small smile, short stubby arms and legs, a diagonal sash across the chest.

Image 1: card front (Dumo-style hand reaching in)
[preamble] Khybi seen from the front, body cropped at the left edge, right arm raised and reaching to the right holding a smartphone, three small curved tap-signal arcs coming off the phone. Body color #F5F7F6 (off-white), face patch and phone screen #1C4D4A (deep teal), ear tips and sash #E8622D (coral). Composition leaves the right half empty.

Image 1b: same pose, inverted for the cream card
[preamble] Same as Image 1 but body color #1C4D4A (deep teal), face patch #F5F7F6, ear tips and sash #E8622D.

Image 2: card back (two Khybis peeking over a line)
[preamble] Two Khybi heads side by side peeking up over the bottom edge, only the heads, ears and the top of the shoulders visible, the right one has one hand raised in a wave. Left Khybi has closed happy eyes, right Khybi has open dot eyes. Body color #1C4D4A, face patches #F5F7F6, ear tips #E8622D. Wide composition, lots of empty space above.

Image 2b: same, inverted for the teal card
[preamble] Same as Image 2 but body color #F5F7F6 and face patches #1C4D4A.

Notes
- Keep the color count to 3: body, face, accent. The printer wants flat spot colors.
- If Recraft adds outlines or shading, add "no outline, no shadow, flat fill only" to the end.
- Generate 4 variations per prompt and pick the one whose pose matches the placeholder on the canvas.
